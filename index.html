01 The House

A house built on cloth, cut and patience.

Kojo Adjei Couture is a made-to-measure house working out of Accra. Nothing here is mass-produced. Every garment begins with a length of hand-loomed cloth and a conversation about the person who will wear it.

The debut collection carries cloth Ghana already knows — strip-woven cotton, deep indigo, quiet burgundy — into a silhouette that is deliberately unhurried. Soft shoulders. Generous sleeves. Trousers cut to move, not to cling.

There is no season to keep up with and no logo to announce. Only cloth, a pattern drafted to one body, and the time it takes to do both properly.

  • i

    Cloth before cut

    We begin with the loom. Ghanaian weavers produce the cloth each piece is built on — the design follows the cloth, never the other way round.

  • ii

    Small by design

    Numbered editions and one-off lengths. No wholesale, no repeats, no second identical piece waiting on a rail.

  • iii

    Cut in Accra

    Drafted, fitted and finished in the atelier. The whole garment stays in the hands of the people who started it.

03 Atelier

Cloth first. Cut second.
Everything else is noise.

  • The cloth

    Strip-woven cotton from weavers across Ghana — hand-dyed indigo, madder, and a red the house keeps returning to. Cut in short, numbered lengths.

  • The cut

    Soft-shouldered shirts, dropped sleeves, cropped trousers. Drafted flat to one body and fitted twice before a single button goes on.

  • The hand

    Buttonholes, edge stitching and the final press are done by hand in the Accra atelier. The garment leaves only when it hangs right.

04 Bespoke

How a garment is made here.

Four steps, one fitting room, no production line. Appointments are private and run through WhatsApp from the first message.

  1. 01

    The conversation

    We start with the occasion, the climate and the clothes you actually reach for. No mood board required — a few honest answers are enough.

    45 minutes
  2. 02

    The cloth

    You choose from the house’s current lengths. Each is limited and numbered, and once a length is gone it does not return.

    In the atelier
  3. 03

    Draft & fit

    A pattern is drafted to you, cut, and brought to a first fitting. Everything is adjusted on the body, never on a hanger.

    Two to three weeks
  4. 04

    Finish

    Hand-finished, pressed, and delivered in a cotton garment bag — collected in Accra or sent onward.

    Delivered
