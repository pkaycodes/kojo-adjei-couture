Privacy policy

Kojo Adjei Couture is a made-to-measure house in Accra, Ghana. This page explains what happens to information you share with us through this preview site.

1. Who we are

This site is operated by FK Media Group on behalf of Kojo Adjei Couture, Accra, Ghana. You can reach the house on WhatsApp at +233 50 434 3113 or through fkmediagroup.com.

2. What we collect

This is a preview site. There are no accounts, no checkout and no payments here.

  • Enquiries you send us. If you use the enquiry form or a WhatsApp link, you choose what to share — typically your name, email address, message, and later any measurements or fitting details. The form is composed in your browser and opened in WhatsApp; nothing is sent to a server on this site.
  • Your cookie choice. One entry in your browser's local storage records whether you accepted or declined the notice, so we do not ask again.
  • Technical data. Our host, GitHub Pages, records standard request logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested. Google Fonts is loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address when the fonts are fetched.

3. What we do not collect

No advertising cookies, no tracking pixels and no analytics profiles. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

4. How we use your information

We use what you send us to answer your enquiry, to tell you when the collection launches if you asked us to, and to prepare a commission if you go ahead. We rely on your consent and on our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.

5. Who else sees it

Only the people who need to answer you. Messages sent through WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy terms. Our site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes request logs on our behalf. We do not pass your details to anyone else unless the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months so we can pick up a conversation about a future collection, then deleted. If you become a client, order and fitting records are kept for as long as needed to service the garment and to meet our accounting obligations.

7. Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you may ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent to being contacted. Message the atelier on WhatsApp and we will action the request. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

8. Children

This site is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if you believe a child has sent us details, contact us and we will delete them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date below. Material changes affecting how we use information you have already shared will be mentioned in the reply to your enquiry.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: WhatsApp +233 50 434 3113, or FK Media Group at fkmediagroup.com.
